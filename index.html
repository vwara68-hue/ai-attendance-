Three steps, no roll call

  1. Capture

    A camera or scanner at the door sees each person as they arrive.

  2. Recognize

    The AI matches them to your enrolled list and marks them present or late.

  3. Review

    Check the live sheet, fix any mistake in one tap, and export the record.

Time back for the people who matter

Saves minutes every session

Nobody spends the start of a lesson or meeting on names.

Fewer errors, no proxy check-ins

Matching a person to a record is harder to fake than signing a sheet.

Live and searchable

See who is in right now, or look up any past date or person.

Reports ready to share

Export daily, weekly, or per-person summaries when you need them.

Built with consent and privacy

People are enrolled only with their permission. Data is used for attendance and nothing else, access is limited to authorized staff, and anyone can ask to have their data removed.
